# Project notes

- Fictional B2B SaaS business scenario.
- 120 synthetic accounts; no real company/customer data.
- Dashboard: portfolio KPIs, license allocation, adoption, usage and account exploration.
- Health index: transparent weighted heuristic, not a churn probability.
- Next-step suggestions: deterministic business rules for human review.
- Optional OpenAI assistant: receives aggregate metrics and up to 20 synthetic account records from current filters; no external actions.
- Inspired by general B2B software product-adoption and customer-monitoring themes; not an Eliis deliverable.
- IPT automation experience is described separately and is not part of this app.
