# Project notes

## Business problem
Customer teams need an early, prioritized view of accounts showing declining product adoption.

## Fictional operating assumptions
- 120 B2B customers.
- Three segments: Enterprise, Mid-market and SMB.
- Usage signals: active seat rate, weekly sessions, feature adoption, support tickets, recency of login, 30-day usage change and NPS.
- Illustrative outcome: a simulated indicator of churn within 90 days.

## Decision workflow
1. Filter the customer portfolio.
2. Review high-risk accounts.
3. Inspect the signals for an individual account.
4. Use the recommended next action as a starting point for human review.
5. Record the outcome in a future CRM integration (not implemented in this prototype).

## Important distinction
The risk estimate is generated by a machine-learning model trained on synthetic labels. The action recommendation is a transparent rule-based layer. The prototype is not an autonomous agent and does not claim production-grade predictive performance.
