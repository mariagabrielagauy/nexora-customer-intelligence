# NEXORA — Portfolio case brief

## One-line pitch
I built a fictional B2B SaaS customer-intelligence prototype that combines product-adoption signals, account prioritization and an optional natural-language AI assistant.

## The challenge
Customer and product teams may have many usage and licensing indicators but lack a single, practical view of which accounts need attention and why.

## The approach
- Model a fictional portfolio of 120 accounts with synthetic usage, licensing and engagement indicators.
- Build an interactive Streamlit dashboard with filters and executive KPIs.
- Create an explainable weighted health index rather than presenting an unvalidated score as a churn probability.
- Add transparent, rule-based next-step suggestions.
- Offer an optional LLM assistant to explore filtered data using natural language.

## What I learned / demonstrated
- Translating a business question into a data workflow.
- Selecting and organizing product and customer indicators.
- Building a usable analytical interface.
- Combining deterministic logic with an LLM feature.
- Communicating limitations and separating synthetic demonstration data from real business data.

## Connection to my experience
My B2B software experience at Eliis exposed me to software usage indicators, customer indicators, product performance, user support and QA. These themes informed the problem framing only. NEXORA is an independent fictional project and is not an Eliis product or deployment.

## Honest scope
The dataset is synthetic. The health index is heuristic, not a validated churn model. Recommendations are prompts for human review. The LLM assistant is optional and requires an API key. The prototype does not integrate with a CRM or take external actions.
